# Commits

Create overlay commits with `pkgdev commit` and a custom, focused message formatted to 72 characters.

Use `pkgdev commit` for commits in this repository. Supply an explicit
message instead of relying on an automatically generated summary.

Keep the message focused on the concrete change and its purpose. Name the
affected package or topic in the subject, and use the body for relevant
behavior, rationale, and validation details.

Keep the subject at most 72 characters. Separate it from the body with a
blank line, and wrap body paragraphs to at most 72 characters per line.
